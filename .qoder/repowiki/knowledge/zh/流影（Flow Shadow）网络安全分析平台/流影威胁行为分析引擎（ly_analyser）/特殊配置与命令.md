需在CentOS-7上先安装gcc/cmake/bison/flex/json-c-devel/boost-devel/libcurl-devel/libpcap-devel/mariadb-devel/httpd/ntp，再按README顺序编译cgicc-3.2.16、cppdb-0.3.1、protobuf-3.8.0及解压tf.tar.gz/tf_lib.tar.gz至/usr/local/{include,lib}并建立so软链；随后在`src/common/`和`src/agent/`分别执行`make && make install`，并在`src/nfdump/`执行`./configure && make`后拷贝`bin/nfcapd bin/nfdump`到`/Agent/bin`；运行时需创建`/Agent`与`/data/flow/3`软链，配置httpd监听10081并启用CGI，启动`nfcapd -w -D -l /data/flow/3 -p 9995`接收流量，并以cron每5分钟调度`/Agent/bin/extractor`。
